Privacy policy

LogPal · Last updated 13 October 2026

LogPal is a calorie and nutrition tracker. This page says what it stores, where, and what you can do about it. There is no advertising in LogPal, nothing about you is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone except as described below.

Using LogPal without an account

You can use LogPal without signing in. In that mode everything you enter stays on your device, in the app's own storage. It never leaves the device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes it.

What an account stores

If you create an account, the following is stored so it can follow you between devices:

  • Your email address, and a password if you chose one. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Your username and first name, and a last name if you gave one.
  • Profile details used to work out your targets: sex, date of birth, height, weight, body type, activity level and goal.
  • What you log: foods, exercise, water, weight, body measurements, fasts, custom foods, meals and recipes.
  • Who you follow and who follows you.

This data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database, and it is protected so that only your own account can read or change your rows.

Signing in with Google

If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and basic profile, and nothing else. We do not receive your Google password and we cannot access anything else in your Google account. Google's own privacy policy covers what Google does.

Your username is public

Usernames exist so that friends can find each other, so any user can search for yours. Your diary is never visible to anyone. If you choose to share your name, your streak or your calories with people who follow you, only the figures you switched on are shared, and only with them. A private account must approve each follower first.

Food lookups

Most food searches run entirely on your device against a database built from USDA FoodData Central. When you scan a barcode, or search for a product that is not in that database, the barcode or the words you typed are sent to Open Food Facts, a public community database, to look it up. Nothing that identifies you is sent with them.

Camera and photos

The barcode scanner and meal scan use the camera only while that screen is open. Barcode frames are decoded on your device and are not stored. A meal photo is used to show you the plate while you describe it; the photo itself is not uploaded and is discarded when you leave the screen. Two photos are kept if you choose to take them: a profile picture, which friends you have accepted can see, and a small photo of your hand taken during setup, which LogPal uses as a size reference for portions. Both are stored with your profile (on your device, and in your account if you are signed in), the hand photo is never shown to anyone, and either can be removed or replaced at any time from Settings.

Apple Health

In the iOS app you can choose to connect LogPal to Apple Health. If you do, LogPal reads your step counts, weight and workouts to show them in your diary, and writes the water, weight and nutrition you log so Health has the full picture. What is read from Health is stored with the rest of your diary: on your device, and in your account if you are signed in, so it appears on your other devices. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone else. You can end the connection at any time in the Health app (your picture, then Apps, then LogPal), and deleting your account deletes everything that was imported.

What we do not collect

LogPal has no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no third-party tracking. It does not read your contacts, your location or Apple Health.

Deleting your data

From inside the app, Settings → Account offers two things: Clear all data empties your diary and history but keeps the account, and Delete my account removes the account itself along with everything stored under it, including your username. Both take effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Children

LogPal is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything material will be pointed out in the app.
