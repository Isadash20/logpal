## Why these words

People find a calorie app by typing what they want it to do, not its name. Apple indexes the
**name, subtitle and keyword field** (not the description) for search, and does not need a word
repeated across them. So the three fields share no words, and together cover the searches that
matter: calorie counter, calorie tracker, macro tracker, food diary, nutrition, barcode scanner,
meal planner, intermittent fasting, water tracker, weight loss, step counter, Apple Health.
"Free" and "no ads" go in the subtitle and the first line of the description, because that is the
first thing someone compares against the paid incumbents. The audience is adults who want to log
what they eat, drink and do, against goals they set themselves, with friends they choose. Nothing
in the listing promises medical outcomes, diagnosis or treatment, which keeps it clear of
Guideline 1.4.1 and 5.1.1(ix).

## Description (4000)

LogPal is a free calorie counter and macro tracker with no ads and no subscription. Log what you eat in seconds, see it against goals you set, and watch the trend over weeks.

LOG FOOD FAST
• Search a database of hundreds of thousands of foods, built from USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts
• Scan a barcode and the product appears, portion and all
• Photograph a Nutrition Facts label and LogPal reads the numbers for you
• Snap a plate with Meal Scan and describe it; portions start from "1 serving" so you never have to know what a serving weighs
• Save meals and recipes you eat often and log them in one tap

SEE WHERE YOU STAND
• Calories, protein, carbs and fat against your targets, updated as you log
• A Home board of widgets you arrange yourself: macros, water, steps, sleep, weight, fasting
• A nutrition tab for fibre, sugar, sodium, vitamins and minerals
• Weekly review that reads your week back to you in plain words

TRACK MORE THAN FOOD
• Water in cups, millilitres or ounces, with a goal that follows your body and your day
• Steps, sleep and weight, typed in or pulled from Apple Health
• Exercise with calories estimated from how far and how high you went, not a flat number
• Intermittent fasting timer with 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD and your own windows

A PLAN THAT FITS YOU
• Tell LogPal your goal (lose, maintain, gain or recomp) and how fast, and it sets calorie and macro targets from your body and activity
• Change any target by hand at any time
• Metric or US units, eight languages

APPLE HEALTH
• Optional, off until you turn it on
• Reads steps, weight and workouts from Health, including Strava, Garmin and any app that writes there
• Writes water, weight and nutrition back so Health has the full picture
• Health data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers

FRIENDS, ON YOUR TERMS
• Follow friends and see how their day is going
• Private by default: only friends you accept see anything. Choose to show percentages of goals, or the numbers themselves, to everyone
• Crews for group accountability and weekly challenges on steps, water and calories
• No feed, no comments, no messaging. Just the numbers you chose to share

YOUR DATA
• Works without an account, entirely on your device
• Sign in to sync across iPhone, iPad and the web
• Delete your account and everything with it in two taps, any time
• No ads, no analytics trackers, no selling data

LogPal is a general wellness app. It does not give medical advice and is not a substitute for a doctor or dietitian.

## App Privacy (nutrition labels) — what to declare

Data collected, linked to the user (only when signed in):
- Contact Info → Email Address: App Functionality (account)
- Health & Fitness → Health (when Apple Health is linked), Fitness (steps, workouts): App Functionality
- User Content → Photos (profile photo, hand photo): App Functionality
- User Content → Other User Content (food diary, weight, water): App Functionality
- Identifiers → User ID: App Functionality
Data not collected: Location, Contacts, Browsing/Search History, Purchases, Financial Info, Diagnostics, Usage Data, Identifiers for tracking.
Tracking: No. The app contains no analytics or ad SDKs.
