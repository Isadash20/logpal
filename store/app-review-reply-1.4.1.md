Hello,

Thank you for the review. Build 1.1 (38) adds citations for every health figure the app calculates or recommends.

Where to find them:

1. Settings → About → "Sources and references" opens a Sources screen that lists, for each calculation, how it is worked out and the published source behind it, with a link that opens in Safari. It covers the calorie target (Mifflin-St Jeor 1990; FAO/WHO/UNU 2004 activity factors; Hall et al. 2011), the protein/carbohydrate/fat split (National Academies Dietary Reference Intakes 2005; Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2020-2025; ISSN 2017), the water target (National Academies DRI for water 2005; ACSM 2007), exercise calories (2011 Compendium of Physical Activities), the step and sleep targets (Paluch et al. 2022; AASM/SRS 2015), intermittent fasting (de Cabo & Mattson, NEJM 2019; Liu et al., NEJM 2022), the nutrition score (FDA Daily Values; WHO sugars guideline 2015), and the food databases (USDA FoodData Central; Open Food Facts).

2. Each screen that shows a recommendation now has a "Where these numbers come from" link directly under the figure, which opens the matching section of the Sources screen: Settings → Plan (daily targets), Your plan (the BMR/maintenance arithmetic), Water, Exercise, Steps, Sleep, Fasting, and the recipe health score. The onboarding plan step links to the calorie and water sources inline.

3. The Sources screen and the onboarding plan both state that the figures are estimates and general information, not medical advice, and advise anyone with a medical condition, who is pregnant, or on medication that depends on food to consult a clinician.
